Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains what information Harbor collects and how it's used. Harbor is operated by Youssef El Mansour.

Information we collect

When you sign up for Harbor, we collect the store name, contact email, and knowledge base content (product info, policies, FAQs) you provide during setup. When your customers use your chat widget, their messages are processed to generate assistant replies but are not used to train any shared or public model.

Payment information

Subscription payments are handled entirely by Paddle.com, our payment provider and merchant of record. Harbor does not directly collect or store your card details — Paddle processes and secures all payment data under its own privacy policy.

How we use information

We use the information you provide to operate your assistant, process billing, and respond to support requests. We do not sell your data to third parties.

Data storage

Your knowledge base content and account details are stored securely using Supabase. Chat conversations may be logged to help improve service quality and for your own reference.

Third-party services

Harbor relies on third-party infrastructure to operate, including Paddle (payments), Supabase (database), Vercel (hosting), and Anthropic (AI processing). Each of these providers has its own privacy practices governing data they process on our behalf.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account data at any time by contacting us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of Harbor after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact
